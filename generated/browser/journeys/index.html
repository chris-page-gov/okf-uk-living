# Citizen journeys

End-to-end journeys organized around what a person is trying to achieve, not
around departmental boundaries.

The three implemented priority journeys are
[missed rubbish collection](missed-rubbish-collection.md),
[learning to drive through a speeding exception](learning-to-drive-speeding.md)
and [death and bereavement through estate administration](death-bereavement-estate.md).
