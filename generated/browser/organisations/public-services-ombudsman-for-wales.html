# Public Services Ombudsman for Wales

The Ombudsman publishes the external route selected for the
[Wales context](../jurisdictions/wales.md). Its guidance says to complain to
the responsible organisation first and confirms that the provider must fall
within its jurisdiction. This record does not predict whether a complaint
will be accepted or upheld.
