# Belfast missed bin collection

[Belfast City Council](../organisations/belfast-city-council.md) provides this
local example in the selected
[Northern Ireland context](../jurisdictions/northern-ireland.md). It belongs to
the normalized [report-missed-rubbish family](report-missed-rubbish-collection.md).

Observed official conditions on 2026-08-07 include waiting until after 7pm on
collection day. The page says an overfull or heavy bin, wrong items, a damaged
bin or a bin without its liner does not count as missed. It provides online and
telephone reporting and identifies Bryson Recycling for a specified recycling
service, making a private-sector delivery dependency visible without changing
the council route. These are Belfast observations only.

The council complaint procedure covers complaints about council services or a
contractor. Its final decision can lead to the
[Northern Ireland Public Services Ombudsman](../organisations/northern-ireland-public-services-ombudsman.md),
subject to completing the council process and current NIPSO rules.
