# Northern Ireland speeding notice and court route

The [Northern Ireland Road Safety Partnership](../organisations/northern-ireland-road-safety-partnership.md)
is named in the selected camera-notice guidance. The registered keeper is
required to identify the driver and must return the form within the time
allotted on the notice. A mistake is directed to the notice contact rather
than being resolved by this bundle.

Later branches can include a course invitation, conditional offer or court
hearing, but this record predicts none of them. The fixed-penalty source gives
notice-specific payment conditions and allows a court hearing within the
stated time. The [NICTS](../organisations/northern-ireland-courts-and-tribunals-service.md)
and current court documents govern that process; the appeal source identifies
the Northern Ireland conviction and sentence appeal boundary.

No Great Britain section 172, England and Wales single justice, or Scottish
prosecutor process is assigned to the
[Northern Ireland context](../jurisdictions/northern-ireland.md).

All facts were observed on 2026-08-07 and remain subject to the
[evidence limitations](../evidence/learning-to-drive-speeding-sources.md).
