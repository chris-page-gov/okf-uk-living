# Cardiff missed collection

[Cardiff Council](../organisations/cardiff-council.md) provides this local
example in the selected [Wales context](../jurisdictions/wales.md). It belongs
to the normalized
[report-missed-rubbish family](report-missed-rubbish-collection.md).

Observed official conditions on 2026-08-07 include reporting within 48 hours
and having presented the waste by 6am. The page identifies reasons a collection
may not count as missed, including contamination, incorrect presentation, an
overfull container, blocked access or damage. During some announced
disruptions, the page can say that no report is needed. These are Cardiff facts,
not a general Welsh deadline or rule.

The council says a service issue must first be reported through the service
route before its corporate complaint route is used. The selected external
route is the
[Public Services Ombudsman for Wales](../organisations/public-services-ombudsman-for-wales.md),
after the organisation complaint and subject to current jurisdiction.
