# Notice-issuing police force in Great Britain

This is a normalized authority role, not a claim that one police body issues
every [Great Britain speeding notice](../services/great-britain-speeding-notice.md).
The actual synthetic or real notice supplies the issuer identity, reference,
response channel and deadline. The bundle does not infer those values.
