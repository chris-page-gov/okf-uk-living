# Evidence

Indexes of authoritative sources, observations, claim evidence, rights
decisions, coverage denominators and known omissions.

An official source can still be stale or unavailable. Authority, derivation,
freshness, availability and coverage remain separate evidence dimensions.

## Implemented evidence sets

- [Missed rubbish collection source set](missed-rubbish-collection-sources.md)
- [Learning to drive and speeding source set](learning-to-drive-speeding-sources.md)
- [Death, bereavement and estate source set](death-bereavement-estate-sources.md)

The repository-wide [licensing decisions](../LICENSE_DECISIONS.md) and
[machine-readable rights register](../source/rights-decisions.v1.yaml) record
dated evidence for every registered source family. Source content is linked and
summarized, not redistributed.
