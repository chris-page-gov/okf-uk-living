# Service families

Canonical descriptions of public-service families. A service family is not a
claim that an identically named transaction, provider, rule or eligibility
test applies everywhere in the United Kingdom.

Each implemented record must state its jurisdiction, responsible provider,
source, observation time, requirements, evidence, channels, outcomes and
redress routes where known.
