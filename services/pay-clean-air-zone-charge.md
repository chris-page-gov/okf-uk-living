# Pay a clean air zone charge

## Place in the process

This topic sits within **Own and use a road vehicle** in the **Public and private transport** life-course
domain. What comes before depends on the person's situation and nation; the first
safe step is to select the current official route rather than assume one UK process.

## What this family covers

Check the vehicle and journey against the current official zone service, pay where required and retain payment or exemption evidence.

The ordinary discovery journey is to select the jurisdiction-specific route, follow
