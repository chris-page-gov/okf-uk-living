# Northern Ireland car-learning and licensing route

The [Driver and Vehicle Agency](../organisations/driver-and-vehicle-agency-northern-ireland.md)
provides the selected licensing and testing stages in the
[Northern Ireland context](../jurisdictions/northern-ireland.md). This is not a
DVLA or DVSA route.

The observed sequence requires provisional entitlement before learning. The
Northern Ireland learner guidance independently states supervisor, vehicle,
insurance and plate conditions and a 45 mph learner limit. A theory-test pass
precedes the practical-test booking in the selected car path. The practical
test produces a result; after a pass, DVA must receive the test-pass handoff,
normally through the examiner's automatic upgrade where applicable or through
the stated claim process.

Private training and [insurance](../organisations/motor-insurer.md) remain
separate dependencies. No GB motorway-learning rule or GB test service is
silently assigned to Northern Ireland.

All facts were observed on 2026-08-07 and remain subject to the
[evidence limitations](../evidence/learning-to-drive-speeding-sources.md).
