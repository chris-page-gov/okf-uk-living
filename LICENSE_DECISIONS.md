# Licensing decisions

No repository-wide licence is inferred by this scaffold. Publication remains
blocked until the owner records and approves:

- the licence for repository-authored code;
- the licence for repository-authored documentation and ontology terms;
- the rights basis for every acquired source family;
- attribution requirements for Open Government Licence material;
- the CC BY 4.0 terms applying to CPSV-AP materials;
- the applicable Open Referral UK/HSDS terms;
- whether source content is linked, summarized or redistributed;
- redistribution constraints for snapshots and generated projections; and
- the reviewer, decision date and evidence for each determination.

The initial research overview links to and summarizes public sources; it does
not claim that linked material is owned or licensed by this repository.
