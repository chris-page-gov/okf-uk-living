# Ontology

Planned governed vocabulary for life stages, events, situations, user needs,
services, transactions, roles, providers, jurisdictions, rules, requirements,
evidence, channels, costs, outcomes, time and redress.

The ontology will map to selected terms from CPSV-AP, Open Referral UK, SKOS,
RDFS, PROV-O and SHACL while retaining project-specific citizen-journey terms
only where the source standards do not supply them.

## Implemented slice modules

- [Governed cross-domain predicate registry](governed-predicates.v1.yaml)

- [Missed rubbish collection model](missed-rubbish-collection.md)
- [Learning to drive and speeding model](learning-to-drive-speeding.md)
- [Death, bereavement and estate model](death-bereavement-estate.md)

The predicate registry is MIT-licensed repository vocabulary. Its mappings to
CPSV-AP, SKOS, Schema.org, Dublin Core and PROV are references; neither the
registry nor the SHACL-style family shapes claim formal conformance.
